About Hikmah Communication

Our Mission


Hikmah Communication exists to bring honest, thoughtful, and contextual news coverage to the MYSverse community. We believe that informed citizens make better decisions, and that good journalism — grounded in Islamic values of truth, fairness, and wisdom — is a form of public service.

Our Vision


To become the most trusted and respected news organization in the MYSverse — a platform where journalism is done with wisdom, where every story serves the public interest, and where the community can find reliable information on the issues that shape their virtual nation.
